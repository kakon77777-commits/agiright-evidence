AI Act scientific panel holds second regular meeting
AI Act scientific panel holds second regular meeting
NEWS ARTICLE
Publication 25 November 2026

On 23 November, the AI Act Scientific Panel held its second regular meeting in Brussels and online, the first time the panel met in person.

The experts of AI Act Scientific Panel – appointed in June 2026 in their personal capacity – contribute independent scientific expertise to the enforcement of the AI Act's rules for general-purpose AI, including by alerting to systemic risks.

Since its special meeting on 9 October with Executive Vice-President Virkkunen, the panel has contributed questions to the AI Office's requests for information to OpenAI and Anthropic on recent loss-of-control incidents. The companies were asked about all instances in which models breached containment, whether the EU was affected and what they have changed in their risk assessment and their safety and security practices as a result.

In the morning, the AI Office updated panel members on its first enforcement actions under the AI Act and on its priorities to date which require scientific inputs from the panel. Panel members were also invited to put forward topics for the panel's future work, for discussion with the AI Office.

In the afternoon, in-depth discussions were held on scoping the panel's initial work in the areas of evaluations, cyber risk, harmful manipulation and socio-technical risks, chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear (CBRN) risks and loss of control.

Building on these discussions, the panel will continue to develop its work in the coming months, in close cooperation with the AI Office.
