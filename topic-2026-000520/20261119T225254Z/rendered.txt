Reps. Whitesides, Moran, and Beyer Launch First Congressional Caucus Focused on AI Safety and Innovation
Home
Press Releases
Bipartisan congressional caucus will champion AI safety legislation, address emerging security threats, and help Congress respond more quickly to AI safety incidents

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Reps. George Whitesides (D-CA), Nathaniel Moran (R-TX), and Don Beyer (D-VA), announced the launch of the House AI Safety and Innovation Caucus, the first congressional caucus focused specifically on addressing AI safety and security risks posed by increasingly advanced AI systems while ensuring the United States remains a global leader in innovative AI technologies.

As AI capabilities accelerate, so do the potential consequences of catastrophic misuse, loss of control over advanced systems, and threats to national security. In July, an experimental OpenAI agent escaped its testing environment and breached the external platform Hugging Face to cheat on an evaluation, without any human directing it to. Anthropic also disclosed three separate incidents in which its AI models escaped testing environments and gained unauthorized access to external organizations during cybersecurity evaluations. These recent security incidents have underscored the need for Congress to better understand these risks and develop policy solutions to prevent these incidents before emerging threats become larger problems. By addressing these challenges proactively, Congress can help create the conditions for continued American innovation and ensure the public can confidently benefit from advances in AI.

“AI is advancing faster than ever before, and Congress needs to move just as quickly,” said Rep. George Whitesides. “Now is the time to build bipartisan consensus and advance commonsense guardrails we know will reduce the most serious risks. Americans are rightly concerned about where this technology is heading, but too often the debate in Washington is framed as a choice between doing nothing and banning AI altogether. That’s a false choice. We have an opportunity to pursue smart, targeted solutions that protect the public while allowing American innovation to thrive. I co-founded this caucus with Reps. Moran and Beyer to fight for that balance, because safe, secure, and controllable AI is the only path to unlocking its full promise for humanity.” 

“We don’t have to choose between innovation and safety,” said Rep. Nathaniel Moran. “We can, and we should, do both. Artificial intelligence affects every American, and it should not be a partisan issue. This caucus gives us a place to find the areas of consensus, set partisanship aside, and put smart, targeted policies in place so we can innovate while we keep people safe. Congress needs to be prepared, and I’m proud to be one of the co-chairs leading that work.” 

“AI has immense potential to improve our lives and strengthen American innovation, but that progress can’t come at the expense of our national security or public safety,” said Rep. Don Beyer. “Recent cyberattacks involving AI agents and growing warnings from leaders and researchers at the companies developing these technologies make clear that the risks are real and growing. We can’t afford to wait for a major catastrophe before putting meaningful safeguards in place. I’m proud to join the AI Safety Caucus and work with my colleagues to advance responsible legislation that strengthens oversight, security, and transparency while ensuring AI is developed and deployed safely and responsibly.” 

Specifically, the House AI Safety and Innovation Caucus will:

Champion AI safety legislation: Develop legislative proposals and endorse existing bipartisan bills that establish thoughtful, targeted guardrails against the most serious AI risks while supporting continued innovation and American technological leadership.
Rapidly respond to AI incidents: Convene AI developers, technical experts, and government officials to brief Members and staff when significant AI safety incidents or developments occur and identify potential policy responses.
Equip congressional offices: Keep Members and staff informed about emerging AI developments, connect them with outside experts, and provide resources to navigate complex AI policy decisions.
Elevate AI safety publicly: Raise public awareness and make AI safety a national priority through public statements, op-eds, and press events.
Shape Congress’ AI agenda: Help develop federal AI safety policy and provide Members outside the committees of jurisdiction with an avenue to contribute to the conversation and legislative process.

By quickly addressing emerging threats, the AI Safety and Innovation caucus will ensure AI can continue to make key improvements in the fields of American science, medicine, productivity, and national security. This new bipartisan caucus will advance thoughtful, targeted guardrails, engaging technical experts and industry leaders, and equip Congress to respond quickly to emerging risks and significant AI incidents while ensuring America continues to lead in AI innovation.

The House AI Safety and Innovation Caucus launched with Reps. George Whitesides (D-CA), Nathaniel Moran (R-TX), and Don Beyer (D-VA) serving as co-chairs. Founding members include Reps. Yassamin Ansari (D-AZ); Greg Casar (D-TX); Yvette Clarke (D-NY); Ben Cline (R-VA); Mike Flood (R-NE); Bill Foster (D-IL); Valerie Foushee (D-NC); Josh Gottheimer (D-NJ); Pat Harrigan (R-NC); Jim Himes (D-CT); Sara Jacobs (D-CA); Mike Kennedy (R-UT); Ro Khanna (D-CA); Sam Liccardo (D-CA); Ted Lieu (D-CA); Zoe Lofgren (D-CA); Celeste Maloy (R-UT); Doris Matsui (D-CA); April McClain Delaney (D-MD); Blake Moore (R-UT); Jay Obernolte (R-CA); Scott Peters (D-CA); Brad Sherman (D-CA); Suhas Subramanyam (D-VA); Ritchie Torres (D-NY); Lori Trahan (D-MA).
