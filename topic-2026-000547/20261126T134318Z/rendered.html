                                 A BILL


 
 To establish a program for the disclosure of research and development 
    programs in the automation of frontier artificial intelligence 
                               research.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``RSI Safety Act of 2026''.

SEC. 2. AUTOMATION OF FRONTIER AI RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT DISCLOSURE 
              PROGRAM.

    (a) Establishment.--
            (1) In general.--The Secretary shall carry out a program, 
        to be known as the ``Automation of Frontier AI Research and 
        Development Disclosure Program'', through which covered 
        developers shall submit disclosures with respect to the 
        automation of the research and development of technologies 
        related to artificial intelligence under this section.
            (2) Office of frontier artificial intelligence research.--
        There is established in the Department of Commerce an office to 
        be known as the ``Office of Frontier Artificial Intelligence 
        Research'' which shall be under the supervision of a Director 
        of the Office appointed by the Secretary.
            (3) Demonstrated expertise.--The Director of the Office 
        shall have demonstrated expertise in the research and 
        development of artificial intelligence.
            (4) Function.--The Secretary of Commerce shall carry out 
        this section through the Director and the Office.
    (b) Covered Developers.--
            (1) Initial qualifications.--Subject to paragraph (2), a 
        developer shall be a covered developer on the date that the 
        following qualifications both first apply with respect to the 
        developer, whether directly by the entity or through an 
        employee, agent, affiliate, or subsidiary of the entity:
                    (A) The developer has used, or has made a 
                determination that the developer intends to use, not 
                less than 10 septillion integer or floating-point 
                operations in the training of an artificial 
                intelligence model.
                    (B) In the immediately preceding 12 months, the 
                developer expended not less than $100,000,000 for the 
                training of artificial intelligence models.
            (2) Adjustments by regulation.--
                    (A) Periodic adjustments.--Not later than 2 years 
                after the date of the enactment of this Act, and every 
                2 years thereafter, the Secretary may prescribe 
                regulations to modify the numbers in subparagraphs (A) 
                and (B) of paragraph (1) if the Secretary determines 
                that such modifications are appropriate to ensure that 
                the qualifications under paragraph (1), as modified by 
                such regulations, identify the developers that carry 
                out research related to the automation of the research 
                and development of technologies related to artificial 
                intelligence.
                    (B) Alternative determination of qualification.--
                Not earlier than 1 year after the date of the enactment 
                of this Act, the Secretary may prescribe regulations to 
                specify requirements based on capability for the 
                registration of covered developers under this 
                subsection, in place of the qualification described in 
                paragraph (1)(A).
                    (C) Effective date of adjustments.--A regulation 
                prescribed under subparagraph (A) or (B) shall take 
                effect--
                            (i) not earlier than 90 days after the date 
                        of the prescription of the regulation if the 
                        purpose of the regulation is to increase the 
                        number of covered developers, but such a 
                        regulation may take effect on a date that is 
                        not earlier than 30 days after the date of the 
                        prescription of the regulation if the Secretary 
                        determines that such a date is necessary; and
                            (ii) not earlier than 180 days after the 
                        date of the prescription of the regulation, for 
                        any other such regulation.
                    (D) CAISSI consultation.--The Secretary shall 
                consult with CAISSI before prescribing a regulation 
                under this paragraph to determine the appropriate 
                adjustments, if any, with respect to qualifications 
                described in paragraph (1).
            (3) Registration.--A covered developer shall--
                    (A) register with the Secretary not later than 30 
                days after becoming a covered developer; and
                    (B) comply with the requirements under this 
                section.
            (4) Cancellation of prior registration.--
                    (A) In general.--The Secretary shall cancel the 
                registration of a covered developer registered under 
                this section, on the application of the covered 
                developer, if the Secretary determines that the covered 
                developer does not meet the requirements for being a 
                covered developer under paragraphs (1) and (2) for the 
                4 calendar quarters preceding the date on which the 
                covered developer makes the application to the 
                Secretary.
                    (B) Effect of cancellation.--A developer with a 
                registration as a covered developer cancelled by the 
                Secretary under this paragraph shall comply with the 
                requirements of a covered developer under this section 
                through the date on which the Secretary cancels the 
                registration.
                    (C) Registration requirement.--A developer with a 
                registration as a covered developer cancelled by the 
                Secretary under this paragraph shall comply with 
                paragraph (3) if the developer meets the requirements 
                for being a covered developer under paragraphs (1) and 
                (2) after the date on which the Secretary cancels the 
                registration.
            (5) Regulations.--
                    (A) In general.--The Secretary shall prescribe 
                regulations necessary to implement and operate the 
                registration of covered developers under this 
                subsection.
                    (B) Date of first prescription.--The Secretary 
                shall carry out subparagraph (A) not later than 180 
                days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
                    (C) Domesticity requirement.--The regulations under 
                this paragraph shall include requirements that a 
                covered developer shall have an agent or affiliate in 
                the United States that shall be responsible for 
                ensuring that the covered developer complies with this 
                section.
            (6) Publication.--The Secretary shall publish from time to 
        time a list of the covered developers registered under this 
        subsection.
    (c) Quarterly Disclosure Requirement.--
            (1) In general.--A covered developer shall submit to the 
        Secretary a disclosure report each calendar quarter with 
        respect to the activities of the covered developer--
                    (A) from the date on which the covered developer 
                becomes a covered developer, for the first report after 
                the covered developer becomes a covered developer; and
                    (B) in the preceding calendar quarter, for any 
                other such disclosure report.
            (2) Content of disclosure report.--
                    (A) Categories.--The disclosure report referred to 
                in paragraph (1) shall contain information with respect 
                to the following categories:
                            (i) Data and training inputs, covering the 
                        curation, filtering, and generation of training 
                        data, reward-signal design, synthetic data, and 
                        data-quality assessment.
                            (ii) Algorithmic and architectural design, 
                        covering architecture, training recipes, 
                        optimizers, scaling configurations, and 
                        inference-time methods.
                            (iii) Experimentation and research 
                        direction, covering the design, configuration, 
                        execution, prioritization, and interpretation 
                        of experiments and training runs.
                            (iv) Safety, evaluation, and oversight 
                        operations, covering the use of covered systems 
                        to design or conduct evaluations, red-teaming, 
                        monitoring, interpretability research, and the 
                        review of the outputs or actions of other 
                        covered systems.
                            (v) Any other technical specification or 
                        matter as prescribed by the Secretary through 
                        regulation.
                    (B) Fields for each category.--The disclosure 
                report referred to in paragraph (1) shall contain, for 
                each category described in subparagraph (A), the 
                following:
                            (i) Autonomous compute shares, consisting 
                        of each of the following shares, stated 
                        separately, of the computing operations 
                        performed during the period covered by the 
                        disclosure report for covered activities in the 
                        category:
                                    (I) The share performed by covered 
                                systems in the substantially autonomous 
                                performance of the covered activities.
                                    (II) The share performed for a 
                                training run, experiment, or evaluation 
                                initiated or configured by a covered 
                                system.
                            (ii) Review-sampling rate, or the share of 
                        the outputs of substantially autonomous 
                        performance of a covered activity that a human 
                        examined substantively, before or after 
                        integration into a broader workflow, excluding 
                        any nominal approval given at a pace that 
                        precludes meaningful understanding.
                            (iii) Unreviewed action share, or share of 
                        actions in the research and development 
                        pipeline executed without explicit human review 
                        before execution.
                            (iv) Closed-loop cycles, or number of 
                        cycles of design, execution, interpretation, 
                        and redesign completed by a covered system 
                        without human intervention during the period 
                        covered by the disclosure report.
                            (v) Oversight status, or the assessment of 
                        the category based on the test of meaningful 
                        oversight under subparagraph (C).
                            (vi) Role characterization, or the 
                        characterization of the roles performed by 
                        covered systems in the category, including 
                        whether a covered system provides a feedback 
                        signal, constructs a training environment, or 
                        performs self-optimization.
                            (vii) Estimates, determined in accordance 
                        with the published schema under subparagraph 
                        (D), of the researcher-equivalent hours 
                        performed by covered systems, the effect of the 
                        covered systems on the pace of research and 
                        development with respect to the category, and 
                        such similar measures as the Secretary may 
                        specify under the schema.
                    (C) Human oversight.--
                            (i) In general.--The disclosure report 
                        referred to in paragraph (1) shall include an 
                        assessment of the state of human review against 
                        a test of meaningful oversight established by 
                        the Secretary.
                            (ii) Content.--The test of meaningful 
                        oversight referred to in clause (i) shall 
                        include the following:
                                    (I) Whether human supervisors can 
                                and do intervene with respect to the 
                                outputs or actions of an artificial 
                                intelligence system before the outputs 
                                or actions are integrated into broader 
                                research and development workflows, 
                                such as a large training run.
                                    (II) Whether human supervision that 
                                permits substantive assessment of each 
                                distinct automated action is feasible 
                                and carried out based on the volume and 
                                rate of outputs from an artificial 
                                intelligence system.
                                    (III) Whether human supervisors 
                                have sufficient information and 
                                resources to verify the safety of an 
                                artificial intelligence system.
                                    (IV) Whether human supervisors 
                                comply with review protocols ensuring 
                                material human control over autonomous 
                                decision-making.
                    (D) Format of disclosure reports.--The disclosure 
                report referred to in paragraph (1) shall be formatted 
                based on a published schema established by the 
                Secretary, with specifications related to units and 
                rules of measurement, including what shall constitute a 
                distinct action, that shall allow for auditing of the 
                information in the report and meaningful comparison of 
                information across multiple disclosure reports from 
                multiple covered developers.
                    (E) Certification.--The disclosure report referred 
                to in paragraph (1) by a covered developer shall 
                include a certification with respect to the accuracy 
                and completeness of the report by--
                            (i) if the covered developer is an 
                        individual, the covered developer; or
                            (ii) if the covered developer is an entity 
                        other than an individual, an individual who is 
                        an officer of the covered developer appointed 
                        by the covered developer.
                    (F) CAISSI consultation.--The Secretary shall 
                consult with CAISSI, in establishing the specifications 
                referred to in subparagraph (D), with respect to the 
                units and rules of measurement and the design of the 
                measures and information to be audited, and CAISSI 
                shall provide such additional technical assistance to 
                the Secretary as the Secretary may require in carrying 
                out this section.
            (3) Date of submission.--A covered developer shall submit 
        the disclosure report for a calendar quarter under paragraph 
        (1) not later than 45 days after the end of the quarter.
            (4) Retention of reports.--A covered developer that submits 
        a disclosure report under this subsection shall maintain a copy 
        of the report and the records used to prepare the report for 
        not less than 5 years after the date of the submission of the 
        report.
            (5) Interim reports.--The Secretary may prescribe 
        regulations requiring a covered developer to submit interim 
        disclosure reports, in accordance with the requirements for a 
        disclosure report under paragraph (1), on a more frequent 
        interval than every calendar quarter.
            (6) Regulations.--The Secretary may prescribe regulations 
        to carry out this subsection.
    (d) Material Change Disclosure Requirement.--
            (1) In general.--A covered developer that discovers or 
        implements a material shift in the automation of the research 
        and development of technologies related to artificial 
        intelligence shall submit to the Secretary a report on the 
        discovery or implementation of the shift not later than 4 
        business days after the date of the discovery or 
        implementation.
            (2) Content of material change discovery or implementation 
        report.--The report on the discovery or implementation of a 
        material shift referred to in paragraph (1) by a covered 
        developer shall include the following:
                    (A) Information required under subsection (c) 
                affected by the discovery or implementation subject to 
                the report, updated to the date of the submission of 
                the report.
                    (B) A description of the discovery or 
                implementation and the shift.
                    (C) A description of such internal responses of the 
                developer filing the report to the discovery or 
                implementation and the shift, such as a change to a 
                review protocol.
                    (D) A certification with respect to the accuracy 
                and completeness of the report by--
                            (i) if the covered developer is an 
                        individual, the covered developer; or
                            (ii) if the covered developer is an entity 
                        other than an individual, an individual who is 
                        an officer of the covered developer appointed 
                        by the covered developer.
            (3) Retention of reports.--A covered developer that submits 
        a report under this subsection shall maintain a copy of the 
        report and the records used to prepare the report for not less 
        than 5 years after the date of the submission of the report.
            (4) Regulations for material shifts.--The Secretary shall 
        prescribe regulations to specify qualitative or quantitative 
        conditions for what shall constitute a material shift in the 
        automation of the research and development of technologies 
        related to artificial intelligence, which shall include the 
        following as examples of such a shift:
                    (A) A change from a human-supervised operation to a 
                closed-loop operation.
                    (B) A change in the type of involvement, such as a 
                change from an artificial intelligence system providing 
                a feedback signal to the artificial intelligence system 
                building a training environment.
                    (C) The first substantially autonomous performance 
                of a covered activity in a category described in 
                subsection (c)(2)(A) by a new architecture or class of 
                artificial intelligence system.
                    (D) A new internal frontier artificial intelligence 
                model that materially advances research and development 
                automation capability.
            (5) De minimis shifts.--The regulations under paragraph (4) 
        may provide that a covered developer shall not be required to 
        submit a report on the discovery or implementation of a de 
        minimis shift if--
                    (A) the regulations specify the conditions required 
                for a shift to be a de minimis shift;
                    (B) the Secretary notifies the appropriate 
                congressional committees of such regulations on the 
                date of the prescription of the regulations; and
                    (C) the shift meets the conditions specified in the 
                regulations not earlier than 30 days after the date of 
                the prescription of the regulations.
    (e) Review by the Secretary.--
            (1) Requests for response.--The Secretary shall review each 
        report submitted under subsection (c) or (d) and may request a 
        response from the covered developer that submitted the report 
        of an apparent error, inconsistency, or omission in the report.
            (2) Responses to requests.--A covered developer that 
        receives a request under paragraph (1) shall submit to the 
        Secretary a response to the request not later than 30 days 
        after the date of receipt.
            (3) Review.--
                    (A) In general.--The Secretary may examine the 
                records of a covered developer if the covered developer 
                does not comply with this subsection or if the 
                Secretary is not satisfied with the response submitted 
                under paragraph (2) to a request under paragraph (1).
                    (B) On-site review.--The Secretary shall carry out 
                the examination of the records of a covered developer 
                under subparagraph (A), through an employee designated 
                by the Secretary, on the premises of the covered 
                developer if the covered developer gives the employee 
                access to the records, which the covered developer may 
                maintain in a secured environment.
                    (C) Subpoena for production of records.--The 
                Secretary may issue a subpoena to a covered developer 
                to require the production of the records of the covered 
                developer if the covered developer does not give the 
                Secretary, or the employee designated by the Secretary 
                under subparagraph (B), access to the records.
                    (D) Aid of courts.--The Secretary may seek 
                compliance or enforcement of a subpoena issued to a 
                covered developer under subparagraph (C) through an 
                order issued by a United States district court with 
                jurisdiction over the covered developer, and the 
                failure of the covered developer to obey such an order 
                shall constitute contempt of such court.
            (4) Biennial audit.--
                    (A) In general.--The Secretary shall conduct an 
                audit of the records used by a covered developer to 
                submit a report under subsection (c) or (d) within 2 
                years after the date of the submission of the first 
                such report and at such regular intervals thereafter 
                not exceeding 2 years during the period in which the 
                covered developer is required to file a report under 
                subsection (c).
                    (B) Final audit.--The Secretary shall conduct an 
                audit of the records used by a covered developer to 
                submit a report under subsection (c) or (d) within 2 
                years after the covered developer ceases to be required 
                to file a report under subsection (c).
                    (C) Examination of records.--The Secretary shall 
                examine the records of a covered developer for an audit 
                under this paragraph, whether on the premises of the 
                covered developer or through the issuance of a 
                subpoena, in accordance with paragraph (3).
                    (D) Errors.--The Secretary shall treat an error 
                discovered or identified in an audit under this 
                paragraph as a ground for further investigation and may 
                not treat the error as a conclusive determination of 
                inaccuracy or deception.
            (5) Corrections.--
                    (A) In general.--If the Secretary determines that a 
                report submitted by a covered developer under 
                subsection (c) or (d) contains an error, the Secretary 
                may require the covered developer to submit to the 
                Secretary a corrected version of the report that 
                addresses the error.
                    (B) Time to file a corrected report.--If the 
                Secretary makes a decision to require a covered 
                developer to submit to the Secretary a corrected 
                version of a prior report under subparagraph (A), the 
                covered developer shall have not less than 30 days to 
                comply with the decision.
                    (C) Review of corrected report.--The Secretary 
                shall review a report that is submitted by a covered 
                developer under subparagraph (A) to be the corrected 
                version of a prior report and may require further 
                submissions from the covered developer if such 
                submissions are necessary for the Secretary to 
                determine whether to accept the report as successfully 
                addressing the error.
                    (D) Treatment of requirement for correction.--The 
                Secretary may not treat the decision to require a 
                covered developer to submit to the Secretary a 
                corrected version of a prior report to address an error 
                in the prior report as a conclusive determination that 
                the covered developer made the error in a knowing or 
                willful manner.
            (6) Referral.--The Secretary may refer a matter related to 
        carrying out this subsection to the Attorney General if the 
        Secretary determines that the matter may relate to a violation 
        of law.
    (f) Disclosure Protections.--
            (1) In general.--An individual employed by, or affiliated 
        with, a covered developer may submit to the Secretary a report 
        alleging any of the following:
                    (A) The inaccuracy of a report under subsection (c) 
                or (d) or a response under subsection (e), in 
                comparison with the records of the covered developer.
                    (B) Failure to submit a report under subsection (c) 
                or (d), a response under subsection (e), or any other 
                filing required under this section.
                    (C) Any misrepresentation or omission related to a 
                report under subsection (c) or (d), a response under 
                subsection (e), or any other filing required under this 
                section.
            (2) Obligation to investigate.--The Secretary shall 
        investigate such matters alleged in a report under paragraph 
        (1) in a manner that shall protect the privacy and 
        confidentiality of the individual who submits the report.
            (3) Prohibition against retaliation.--It shall be unlawful 
        for a covered developer to discharge or in any other manner 
        discriminate against an individual who is employed by, or 
        affiliated with, the covered developer because the individual--
                    (A) submits a report under paragraph (1) or 
                cooperates with the Secretary in an investigation under 
                this subsection; or
                    (B) reports a matter alleged in a report under 
                paragraph (1) to another individual who is employed by, 
                or affiliated with, the covered developer.
            (4) Penalties.--A violation of paragraph (3) shall be 
        treated as a violation of section 15(a)(3) of the Fair Labor 
        Standards Act of 1938, and for the purposes of such Act with 
        respect to such a violation--
                    (A) the individual who is employed by, or 
                affiliated with, the covered developer shall be deemed 
                an employee; and
                    (B) the covered developer shall be deemed an 
                employer.
            (5) Referrals.--The Secretary may make a referral with 
        respect to an allegation of a violation of this section to the 
        Attorney General.
            (6) Civil penalty for failure to register.--The Attorney 
        General may file a civil action in an appropriate United States 
        district court against a covered developer that is required to 
        register as a covered developer under subsection (b) and 
        knowingly and willfully fails to comply with the registration 
        requirements under the subsection, and the court in such action 
        may assess against the covered developer a civil penalty in any 
        amount, not to exceed $100,000 for each day that begins after 
        the period specified in paragraph (3) of the subsection until 
        the covered developer complies with the registration 
        requirements under the subsection.
            (7) Civil penalty for failure to report.--The Attorney 
        General may file a civil action in an appropriate United States 
        district court against a covered developer that knowingly and 
        willfully falsifies or omits material information required in, 
        or knowingly and willfully fails to submit, a report under 
        subsection (c) or (d) or a response under subsection (e), and 
        the court in such action may assess against the covered 
        developer a civil penalty in any amount, not to exceed 
        $5,000,000 with respect to the first imposition of a penalty 
        under this paragraph and $10,000,000 with respect to a 
        subsequent imposition of a penalty under this paragraph.
            (8) Civil penalty for failure to maintain records.--The 
        Attorney General may file a civil action in an appropriate 
        United States district court against a covered developer that 
        knowingly and willfully does not maintain records required 
        under this section, and the court in such action may assess 
        against the covered developer a civil penalty in any amount, 
        not to exceed $5,000,000 with respect to the first imposition 
        of a penalty under this paragraph and $10,000,000 with respect 
        to a subsequent imposition of a penalty under this paragraph.
    (g) Publication.--
            (1) In general.--A filing or submission by a covered 
        developer to the Secretary under this Act, other than a summary 
        under paragraph (4), and the information in such a filing or 
        submission or obtained by the Secretary under subsection (e) or 
        (f) shall be confidential and may not be disclosed to the 
        public, and the filing, submission, or information may not be 
        made available to the public under section 552 of title 5, 
        United States Code.
            (2) Citation for purposes of open foia act of 2009.--This 
        subsection is a statute that specifically exempts certain 
        matters from disclosure under section 552 of title 5, United 
        States Code, as described in subsection (b)(3) of that section.
            (3) Disclosure to other government agencies.--The Secretary 
        may provide to any of the following a copy of a submission by a 
        covered developer under this section, which the recipient may 
        not disclose to any other entity except to the extent permitted 
        by the Secretary:
                    (A) The Attorney General.
                    (B) CAISSI.
                    (C) Such other internal advisers to the Secretary 
                with expertise in technologies related to artificial 
                intelligence.
            (4) Public summaries of filings.--
                    (A) In general.--A covered developer shall include, 
                in a report under subsection (c) or (d) or a response 
                under subsection (e), a summary of the information in 
                the report or response with respect to the category 
                described in subsection (c)(2)(A)(iv) that shall be 
                suitable for, and disclosed to, the public in 
                accordance with this paragraph.
                    (B) Regulations.--The Secretary shall prescribe 
                regulations to specify a standard format of a summary 
                under subparagraph (A) that shall allow for meaningful 
                comparison of information across multiple summaries 
                from multiple covered developers.
                    (C) Public availability.--The Secretary shall make 
                available to the public a summary under subparagraph 
                (A) not later than 30 days after the date that the 
                Secretary receives the summary.
            (5) Reports by the secretary.--
                    (A) Regular report.--Not later than 2 years after 
                the date of the enactment of this Act and every year 
                thereafter, or on a shorter interval determined by the 
                appropriate congressional committees, the Secretary 
                shall submit to the appropriate congressional 
                committees a report based on the aggregate information 
                in the reports under subsection (c) or (d) and 
                corrections to such information, without breaking the 
                confidentiality of each specific covered developer, 
                which shall contain information on industry-wide or 
                cross-developer trends in each of the categories 
                described in subsection (c)(2)(A).
                    (B) Recommendations.--The Secretary shall include 
                in a report under subparagraph (A) specific 
                recommendations for the Congress to consider with 
                respect to the following:
                            (i) Specifying limits, restrictions, or 
                        conditions on the automation of the research 
                        and development of technologies related to 
                        artificial intelligence.
                            (ii) Additional authorities or powers 
                        required by the Secretary to carry out the 
                        purposes of this section.
                    (C) Feasibility of capabilities-based criteria for 
                registration of developers.--Not later than 1 year 
                after the date of the enactment of this Act, the 
                Secretary shall submit to the appropriate congressional 
                committees a report on the feasibility of identifying 
                measures for artificial intelligence systems and models 
                based on capabilities rather than measurements of raw 
                computational power, such as the number of integer or 
                floating-point operations used with respect to such 
                artificial intelligence systems and models.
                    (D) CAISSI assistance.--The Secretary shall consult 
                with CAISSI in preparing a report under this paragraph.
    (h) Exemption From the Paperwork Reduction Act.--Chapter 35 of 
title 44, United States Code, shall not apply with respect to this 
section.
    (i) Effective Date.--
            (1) In general.--The registration requirement under 
        subsection (b) and the disclosure requirements under subsection 
        (c) and (d) shall apply to a covered developer beginning 1 year 
        after the date of the enactment of this Act.
            (2) Deadline for regulations.--The Secretary shall 
        prescribe regulations with respect to subsections (c)(2)(C)(i), 
        (c)(2)(D), (d)(4), and (g)(4), not later than 270 days after 
        the date of the enactment of this Act.
            (3) Failure to prescribe regulations.--In the event that 
        the Secretary shall fail to comply timely with paragraph (2), a 
        covered developer shall comply, as the case may be, with--
                    (A) the requirements under subsection (c), until 
                the Secretary prescribes the regulations under 
                subsections (c)(2)(C)(i) and (c)(2)(D), by using a 
                consistent format chosen by the covered developer, 
                allowing for meaningful comparison of information 
                across multiple reports under the subsection from the 
                covered developer, that shall include the information 
                required under subsection (c)(2)(B), with a description 
                of the methods and measures used with respect to the 
                format, and an assessment of the state of human 
                oversight or review with respect to the matters 
                described in subsection (c)(2)(C)(ii), with a 
                description of the methods and measures used for the 
                assessment;
                    (B) the requirements under subsection (d), until 
                the Secretary prescribes the regulations under 
                subsection (d)(4), by specifying qualitative or 
                quantitative conditions for what shall constitute a 
                material shift in the automation of the research and 
                development of technologies related to artificial 
                intelligence, including with respect to an example 
                described in subsection (d)(4), which the covered 
                developer shall use consistently across all reports by 
                the covered developer under the subsection; and
                    (C) the requirements under subsection (g), until 
                the Secretary prescribes the regulations under 
                subsection (g)(4), by using a consistent format chosen 
                by the covered developer that shall allow for 
                meaningful comparison of information across multiple 
                summaries from the covered developer.
    (j) Definitions.--In this section:
            (1) Appropriate congressional committees.--The term 
        ``appropriate congressional committees'' means the following:
                    (A) The Committee on Energy and Commerce of the 
                House of Representatives.
                    (B) The Committee on Science, Space, and Technology 
                of the House of Representatives.
                    (C) The Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
                Transportation of the Senate.
            (2) Artificial intelligence.--The term ``artificial 
        intelligence'' has the meaning given the term in section 5002 
        of the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020.
            (3) CAISSI.--The term ``CAISSI'' means--
                    (A) the Center for Advancing Innovation and 
                Standards for Super Intelligence;
                    (B) a successor entity of the entity referred to in 
                subparagraph (A); or
                    (C) in the absence of an entity referred to in 
                subparagraph (A) or (B), such advisers to the Secretary 
                or employees of the Department of Commerce with 
                expertise in technologies related to artificial 
                intelligence, as recommended to the Secretary by the 
                Under Secretary of Commerce for Standards and 
                Technology.
            (4) Covered activity.--The term ``covered activity'' means 
        any activity related to research or development in connection 
        with the development of an artificial intelligence system or 
        model by a covered developer and does not include an activity 
        related to routine software engineering that is not directed to 
        the development of a frontier artificial intelligence model.
            (5) Covered developer.--The term ``covered developer'' 
        means a developer required to be registered under subsection 
        (b).
            (6) Covered system.--The term ``covered system'' means an 
        artificial intelligence model, an agent of an artificial 
        intelligence model, or a coordinated set of components related 
        to artificial intelligence, which is capable of carrying out a 
        covered activity.
            (7) Developer.--The term ``developer'' means an entity that 
        carries out the training of artificial intelligence models, 
        whether directly by the entity or through an employee, agent, 
        affiliate, or subsidiary of the entity, and meets any of the 
        following:
                    (A) The entity is--
                            (i) an individual with United States 
                        nationality or lawfully admitted for permanent 
                        residence in the United States under the 
                        Immigration and Nationality Act;
                            (ii) an entity organized under the laws of 
                        the United States or a State; or
                            (iii) an entity ultimately controlled, 
                        directly or indirectly, by an entity described 
                        in this subparagraph.
                    (B) The entity carries out an activity related to 
                the training of artificial intelligence models in the 
                United States.
            (8) Frontier artificial intelligence model.--The term 
        ``frontier artificial intelligence model'' means an artificial 
        intelligence model--
                    (A) with respect to which the amount of integer or 
                floating-point operations described in subsection 
                (b)(1)(A), or the amount determined under subsection 
                (b)(2)(A), has been used in the training of the model; 
                or
                    (B) if applicable, which meets the requirements 
                based on capability under subsection (b)(2)(B).
            (9) Office.--The term ``Office'' means the Office of 
        Frontier Artificial Intelligence Research established under 
        subsection (a).
            (10) Program.--The term ``Program'' means the Automation of 
        Frontier AI Research and Development Disclosure Program 
        established under subsection (a).
            (11) Secretary.--The term ``Secretary'' means the Secretary 
        of Commerce.
            (12) Substantially autonomous performance.--The term 
        ``substantially autonomous performance'' means, with respect to 
        a covered activity, the performance or carrying out of the 
        covered activity, which consists of a sequence of at least 2 
        actions, with one action proceeding to the next based on the 
        determination of a covered system and without a human 
        individual substantively reviewing an action in the sequence 
        and deciding whether to take the next action.
