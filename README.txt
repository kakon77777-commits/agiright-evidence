AGIRight.org source-snapshot evidence (PRIVATE).

Layout: <entry-id>/<UTC capture time>/ with raw.<ext> (HTTP body as served), rendered.html and rendered.txt
(what a browser showed after scripts ran), screenshot.png (full page, 1366 px wide) and meta.json.

These are copies of other publishers' pages. They are kept for our own verification and are NOT committed
to the public repository or deployed. Only hashes, capture time and Wayback links are published
(src/data/snapshots.json -> https://agiright.org/snapshots/index.json).

Produced by scripts/snapshot.mjs. A snapshot shows what a page said when we captured it, not what it said
earlier, and not whether it is true.
