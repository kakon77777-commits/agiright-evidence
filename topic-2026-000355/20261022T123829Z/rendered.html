Our charter is to responsibly develop and maintain advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity.

Anthropic is a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC), a designation that requires us to balance the financial interests of shareholders with the benefit to the broader public. Our Public Benefit Report takes stock of what we are doing to serve the public benefit, and of the work ahead.

Read our 2026 Public Benefit Report
  • Pillar 1The technical problems of AI safety are solved.Powerful AI can be developed and deployed without severe, irreversible harms.
  • Pillar 2AI safety is a universal practice backstopped by law.AI developers everywhere follow safe development and deployment practices, sustained by industry norms and enforced by regulation.
  • Pillar 3AI strengthens democracy and global stability.Democracies hold the lead in AI and use it to better serve the public and promote peace.
  • Pillar 4AI expands prosperity and human flourishing.AI enables unprecedented economic growth, and the resulting prosperity empowers every person to pursue a good life.
  • Pillar 5AI’s benefits are shared by everyone.AI delivers transformative benefits that reach people everywhere, and the opportunities AI creates are distributed.

What impact is AI having on society? On the economy? On individual wellbeing?

How do we instill goals and values into a large language model? How can we better understand how they think and reason? How do we ensure we are building AI safely?

These, and other questions, have animated our work since our founding in 2021.

Our work centers around five pillars. Each describes a condition we want to be true in a world where AI has gone well.Every year, we’ll publish a report that outlines our progress toward these pillars.
