Our charter is to responsibly develop and maintain advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity.

Anthropic is a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC), a designation that requires us to balance the financial interests of shareholders with the benefit to the broader public. Our Public Benefit Report takes stock of what we are doing to serve the public benefit, and of the work ahead.

What impact is AI having on society? On the economy? On individual wellbeing?

How do we instill goals and values into a large language model? How can we better understand how they think and reason? How do we ensure we are building AI safely?

These, and other questions, have animated our work since our founding in 2021.

Our work centers around five pillars. Each describes a condition we want to be true in a world where AI has gone well.
Every year, we’ll publish a report that outlines our progress toward these pillars.

Explore the work so far.

Building advanced AI in a way that is safe, trustworthy, and accountable to the people it affects isn’t a niche concern or a competitive differentiator. It is a necessary step toward making the benefits real. We’re already seeing what’s possible—in research labs, in classrooms, and in the everyday work of people solving hard problems. If we get this right, I believe that some of humanity’s greatest moments await us on the other side.
Daniela Amodei
Co-Founder and President, Anthropic
