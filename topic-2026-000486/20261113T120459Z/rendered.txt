Chairmen Mast, Moolenaar, and Crawford Send Second Request to Jensen Huang and NVIDIA to Brief Congress on the Chinese Intelligence Asset
November 12, 2026
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – House Foreign Affairs Committee Chairman Brian Mast, Select Committee on China Chairman John Moolenaar, and House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence Chairman Rick Crawford sent letters to former NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang, NVIDIA General Counsel Tim Teter, and Under Secretary of Commerce Jeffrey Kessler requesting their appearance before Congress.

“As reports continue to come to light regarding Jensen Huang, NVIDIA, and Megaspeed, there are clear national security concerns that need to be addressed immediately,” said Chairmen Mast, Moolenaar, and Crawford. “We need to know the full extent to which our national security has been put at risk. This situation is a prime example of why Congress must immediately pass the AI OVERWATCH Act, Chip Security Act, MATCH Act, and Remote Access Security Act in the NDAA.”

You can read copies of all three letters below:

Letter to former NVIDIA CEO, Jensen Huang

Letter to NVIDIA General Counsel, Tim Teter

Letter to Under Secretary of Commerce, Jeffrey Kessler
