Neoclouds are being told they won't be able to purchase Nvidia chips if any of the computing capacity will be rented by Chinese companies, sources told CNBC.

Chip sellers communicated the messaging to neoclouds after the announcement, which seeks to close an export controls loophole allowing Chinese companies to rent access to Nvidia's most advanced chips when hosted in data centers overseas, two people familiar with the situation told CNBC.

Nvidia and the U.S. Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
