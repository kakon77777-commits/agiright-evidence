For instance, OpenAI’s voluntary reporting implies that only 0.001-0.01% of internal agentic coding trajectories were escalated for review

Similarly, Anthropic’s recent voluntary reporting indicates that its AI monitors flag 16 16 16 In this context, a “flag” is output marked as potentially harmful by the AI monitoring system. roughly 100,000 transcripts per week, of which about 50 per week are reviewed by human personnel 1 , 51 . These flags amount to roughly one to two in every thousand transcripts [ 1 ] , which implies that human personnel review about 0.0001% of AI agent activity or less.

Anthropic reports that human personnel review on the order of 50 trajectories per week [ 51 ] , which, by a conservative estimate, amounts to less than 0.01% of AI agent activity.

This implies a total of roughly 50-100 million transcripts per week and a human review rate of roughly 0.00005-0.0001%.

No comparable test of the human reviewers downstream of those monitors has yet been run or publicly shared.
