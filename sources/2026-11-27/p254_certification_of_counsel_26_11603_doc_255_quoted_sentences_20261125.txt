On November 19, 2026, the Office of the United States Trustee for the District of Delaware (the "U.S. Trustee") filed the United States Trustee's Objection to the Notic of Designation of Additional Stalking Horse Purchaser and Stalking Horse Supplement for Assets Other Than the Disney Target Assets [Docket No. 202] (the "Objection").

At the Hearing, the Office of the United States Trustee for the District of Delaware (the "U.S. Trustee") raised certain objections to the Motion.

On November 24, 2026, the Debtor filed the Supplemental Notice of Designation of Additional Stalking Horse Purchaser and Stalking Horse Supplement for Assets Other Than the Disney Target Assets [Docket No. 234] (the "Supplemental Notice") and subsequently withdrew the Motion.

Prior to filing the Proposed Order, the Debtor shared copies of the Proposed Order with counsel to the Additional Stalking Horse Purchaser, the U.S. Trustee, the CPO and proposed counsel to the Official Committee of Unsecured Creditors, none of whom object to its entry.

In light of the forgoing, the Debtor prepared a proposed form of order 2 Case 26-11603-BLS Doc 255 Filed 11/25/26 Page 3 of 3 (the "Proposed Order"), attached hereto as Exhibit A, providing that (i) any objections to the designation of Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC as Additional Stalking Horse Purchaser, to the extent not resolved or deemed moot, are overruled; and (ii) designating Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC as Additional Stalking Horse Purchaser and granting the Bid Protections.
