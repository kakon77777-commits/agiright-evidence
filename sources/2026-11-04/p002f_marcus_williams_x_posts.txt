To be clear these incidents don’t represent the models disabling their own monitoring (disabling monitoring in codex would be much harder to do) but the fact that they seem prone to doing at all should be concerning. There is some plausible deniability in these cases, we have not had cases where the model explicitly disables monitoring for overtly misaligned reasons.

Given the volume of events we do not trigger an immediate page for this but we’re working on making it harder to spin up unmonitored inference.

We are also developing evaluations to test whether models might have a propensity to evade misalignment monitoring in particular and exhibit motivated reasoning in order to do so.
