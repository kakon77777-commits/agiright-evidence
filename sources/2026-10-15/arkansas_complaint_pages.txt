COMPLAINT
I. INTRODUCTION
1. Millions of consumers, including those in Arkansas, use ChatGPT to answer questions, solve problems, and make important life decisions. OpenAI encourages that reliance by marketing ChatGPT as safe, trustworthy, and suitable for teenagers, while assuring consumers that safety is "core to our mission." Yet ChatGPT has repeatedly produced unsafe responses.

249. Attorney General Tim Griffin demands a trial by jury on all issues so triable.
VII. REQUEST FOR RELIEF
250. The State of Arkansas, ex rel. Tim Griffin, Attorney General, respectfully requests this Court:
a. Declare that Defendants have engaged in deceptive, unfair, and unconscionable acts and practices against Arkansas consumers in violation of the ADTPA;
b. Enjoin Defendants and their agents, employees, and all other persons acting on their behalf, directly or indirectly, from violating the ADTPA;
c. Require Defendants to provide clear and conspicuous disclosures of the material risks and limitations the Court finds were not adequately disclosed in connection with the marketing, sale, or use of ChatGPT;
d. Issue such orders as may be necessary to prevent the use or employment by the Defendants of the deceptive and harmful practices described herein, which are violations of the ADTPA;
e. Order restitution to consumers who have been injured by Defendants' deceptive practices;
f. Impose civil penalties under Ark. Code Ann. § 4-88-113(a)(3), to be paid to the State by the Defendants in the amount of $10,000.00 per each violation, as set forth above;
g. Impose enhanced civil penalties under Ark. Code Ann. § 4-88-202(a), to be paid to the State by Defendants in the amount of an additional $10,000 per violation for each of Defendants' violations against an elder person or a person with a disability;

h. Issue an order, in accordance with Ark. Code Ann. § 4-88-113(e), requiring Defendants to pay the State's costs in this investigation and litigation;
i. Find that Defendants are jointly and severally liable, under Ark. Code Ann. § 4-88-113(d), for any civil penalties and monetary judgment awarded;
j. Declare that Defendants have violated the Arkansas CTOPPA in several respects, including by collecting the personal information of children and teens in violation of the Arkansas CTOPPA;
k. Enjoin Defendants from collecting and using personal information of children and teens in violation of Arkansas CTOPPA;
l. Enjoin Defendants from committing any other violations of the Arkansas CTOPPA;

n. Enjoin Defendants from creating models capable of recursive self-improvement until they can ensure that such models will be aligned with human values;
o. Enter an injunction requiring Defendants to adopt whatever additional safety measures and protocols the Court deems necessary to protect Arkansans;
p. Award actual, compensatory, and punitive damages;
q. Award statutory damages in the maximum amount permitted by law;
r. Award reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of suit;
s. Award pre-judgment and post-judgment interest; and
t. Order such other just and proper relief to which the State may be entitled.
