US Moves to Bar China’s DeepSeek From Federal Agency Systems (3)
Oct. 20, 2026, 2:09 PM UTC
Michael Shepard
The Trump administration barred civilian US government agencies from using products or services from Chinese artificial intelligence startup DeepSeek , citing a potential threat to national security from the company’s operations in China.
Federal agencies must remove any DeepSeek products from their systems, according to a notice posted Monday by the Department of Homeland Security on the Federal Register website. An accompanying order, dated July 24 and signed by Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin , also bars the firm from participating in federal government contracts.
