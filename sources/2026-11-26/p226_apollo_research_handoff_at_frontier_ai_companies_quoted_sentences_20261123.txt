esearch 2026-11-23 Executive Summary The term “Handoff” captures frontier AI companies’ ongoing delegation of all AI Research & Development (AI R&D) functions, including high-level decisions such as safety assessments and deployment decisions, to internally deployed AI agents.

Handoff is progressing rapidly and will be complete once no human remains in the loop (Figure 1).

Today, AI agents already write over 90% of the code merged into Anthropic’s codebase [1].

AI agents are also leading a rapidly growing share of AI R&D functions (surging from less than 1% to 26% between February and August 2026 alone) [1], meaning that important high-level decisions such as safety assessments and deployment decisions are increasingly handed off to AI agents.

Timelines for Handoff completion are short: frontier AI companies expect to reach this milestone between 2027 and 2028 [3, 4].

HANDOFF OF DECISION-MAKING: DIRECTION-SETTING AND AUTHORIZATION According to public sources, decision-making is the least advanced of the three components of Handoff: human personnel still set higher-level directions [4, 22], and consequential go/no-go decisions still remain under human control [79, 80].17 However, frontier AI companies may soon begin delegating AI R&D decision-making functions to AI agents, such as deciding which research questions to investigate and which experiments to run, and making consequential go/no-go decisions.

Handoff is proceeding largely outside policymakers’ view (§2).

Frontier AI companies disclose information on Handoff voluntarily, sporadically, and each according to its own metrics.

Meanwhile, human personnel review less than 0.01% of internal AI agent activity, which is mostly left to automated AI monitors [2, 1].
