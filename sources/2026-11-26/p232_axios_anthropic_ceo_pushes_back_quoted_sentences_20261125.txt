Anthropic CEO pushes back on Axios reporting on AI and free will

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei on Wednesday rejected the notion that humans should surrender free will to AI, pushing back on Axios' reporting about a growing ideological divide among top AI executives.

Amodei was adamant that he has always advocated for maintaining human control, but people familiar with company discussions say the belief that AI will take over decision-making functions held by humans is common.
