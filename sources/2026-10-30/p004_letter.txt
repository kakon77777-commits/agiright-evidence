         We are writing to ask whether you have actually been soliciting advice from Elon Musk’s
artificial intelligence (AI) chatbot, Grok, in order to help you carry out your official duties as
President of the United States. If so, we are writing to inform you that any prompts that you,
your staff, or advisors in the Executive Office of the President (EOP) have submitted to AI

including, but not limited to, Grok, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, as well as any AI-
generated outputs in response to your prompts, are the sole property of the United States and
legally must be preserved for posterity. 1

the capture of President Nicolás Maduro. Grok advised that President Maduro was a “deeply
unpopular dictator and that many Venezuelans would likely celebrate his downfall.” 3 A few

        The episode is deeply troubling for several reasons.

        First, you consulted Grok—but not Congress—on your military deployment to
Venezuela, circumventing Congress’s constitutional power to declare war and introducing

        This is not optional. Congress passed the Presidential Records Act (PRA) in 1978 to
establish public ownership of records created by presidents and their staff in the course of
discharging their official duties. 20 Pursuant thereto, the President must take “all such steps as
may be necessary to assure that the activities, deliberations, decisions, and policies that reflect
the performance of the President’s constitutional, statutory, or other official or ceremonial duties
are adequately documented.” 21 Presidential records are defined to include:

        [D]ocumentary materials … created or received by the President, the President’s
        immediate staff, or a unit or individual of the Executive Office of the President
        whose function is to advise or assist the President, in the course of conducting
        activities which relate to or have an effect upon the carrying out of the
        constitutional, statutory, or other official or ceremonial duties of the President. 22

AI prompts submitted by you and your staff, as well as the outputs you received, plainly fall
within the definition of presidential records.

       To ensure that the American public’s rights to presidential records are being adequately
protected, we request a closed-door briefing regarding the use of AI chatbot technologies by
President Donald Trump, his staff, and relevant EOP personnel. The White House representative
should be prepared to address, at a minimum, the following:

        1.      The EOP’s document retention policies governing AI‑generated materials,
                including prompts and outputs submitted by personnel subject to the PRA to AI
                chatbot services such as Grok, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity.

                and to what extent, model training may incorporate inputs provided by EOP
                personnel.

        3.      All policies governing EOP personnel use of AI chatbot services, including but

                limitations on the use of non‑enterprise AI chatbot platforms.
