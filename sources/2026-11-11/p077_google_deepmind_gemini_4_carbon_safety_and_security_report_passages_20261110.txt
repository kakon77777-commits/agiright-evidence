To understand if a subsequent version of the model has meaningful new capabilities or material increases in performance relative to the most recent model version subject to a critical capability assessment, we conduct a Material Capability Change (MCC) assessment. We do this by measuring its general capability using GCI v2, our internal General Capability Index modeled on the Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI).1 Like the ECI, GCI v2 combines a model’s performance across many diverse benchmarks into a single overall capability score. We treat a difference in GCI scores as material if it exceeds four months of capability progress, estimated from a line of best fit over frontier models.
