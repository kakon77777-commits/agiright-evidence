We conduct a critical capability assessment prior to the first external deployment4 of a new frontier AI model. For external deployment of subsequent versions of the model, we determine whether a substantial modification has been made and whether a further critical capability assessment is required if (1) the model has meaningful new capabilities or material increases in performance; and (2) we believe such material capability increase could materially undermine the justification for why the risks stemming from the model are acceptable. To understand if a subsequent version of the model has meaningful new capabilities or material increases in performance relative to the last checkpoint subject to a critical capability assessment, we conduct material capability change assessments on various checkpoints upon the completion of a post-training run. These assessments draw on model characteristics, such as its provenance, size, modality, and performance on general capability benchmarks.
