System Card: Claude Fable 5.2 & Claude Mythos 5.2 November 9, 2026 anthropic.com Executive Summary Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 are two configurations of a new large language model from Anthropic. As with previous models in this class, we are releasing it in two forms. Claude Fable 5.2 is available for general use and has additional safeguards that prevent it from performing certain tasks in high-risk, dual-use domains, such as biology and cybersecurity. Claude Mythos 5.2 has more permissive safeguards in these domains. Direct access to Claude Mythos 5.2 is limited to vetted individuals and organizations through our trusted access programs. Claude Mythos 5.2 also powers Claude Security, which is available to all Claude Enterprise customers. Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 advance the frontier in coding, knowledge work, and problem-solving, with improved capabilities for novel mathematical and scientific reasoning. In this system card, we report the results of pre-deployment evaluations in seven areas: Our Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) and Frontier Compliance Framework (FCF) evaluations found that autonomy threat model 1 (misalignment in high-stakes settings) is applicable to Claude Mythos 5.2, as it is to some of our previous AI models. In Section 2.4, we discuss why we believe the level of risk under this threat model remains at the “low” level assessed in our August 2026 Risk Report. On autonomy threat model 2 (automated R&D in key domains), Claude Mythos 5.2 does not seem close to fully substituting for our research staff, and we believe internal usage of the model does not accelerate our aggregate AI R&D progress by a factor of 2. As a result, we do not consider Claude Mythos 5.2 to have met our RSP threshold in this domain (though this assessment is uncertain). We plan to publish more soon on overall trends in acceleration and what they imply under our Responsible Scaling Policy. On chemical and biological risks, Claude Mythos 5.2 is a modest improvement over Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Mythos 5.1 on several evaluations. However, it does not cross our CB-2 threshold.
