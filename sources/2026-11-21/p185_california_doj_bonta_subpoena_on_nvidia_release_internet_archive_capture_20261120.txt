Attorney General Bonta Serves Investigative Subpoena on Nvidia

Press Release
Attorney General Bonta Serves Investigative Subpoena on Nvid&hellip;










Thursday, November 19, 2026
Contact: (916) 210-6000, agpressoffice@doj.ca.gov
OAKLAND — California Attorney General Rob Bonta today served an investigative subpoena on Nvidia related to the news of former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s affair with an alleged Chinese spy. The California Department of Justice is asking Nvidia whether its advanced chips or confidential information may have gone to China and whether Nvidia misled investors and the public. The information received through the subpoena will help the California Department of Justice evaluate whether Nvidia’s actions violated California law.
“The conduct of Nvidia and its former CEO raises bright red flags, including about the ways in which the company may have misled investors. My office is investigating whether Nvidia or Jensen Huang’s actions resulted in China obtaining advanced chips or confidential information — and if Nvidia misled investors and the public about this risk, in violation of the law,” said Attorney General Bonta. “Nvidia is in a messy, transnational situation but the underlying principle of our corporate securities law is clear: Companies cannot mislead their investors.”
