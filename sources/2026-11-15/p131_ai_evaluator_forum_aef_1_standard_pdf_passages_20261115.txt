Defining task completion, scoring rubrics, and other definitions of success or failure. 3.3 Direct access Recommendation: The evaluator ran the evaluations themselves via direct system access. Evaluators should generally maintain direct technical control of the evaluation, rather than relying on staff of the system provider to serve as intermediaries. However, this condition may not always be justified, especially for evaluation methods dealing with highly sensitive types of access, such as those that involve production user data or direct access to proprietary model weights.
