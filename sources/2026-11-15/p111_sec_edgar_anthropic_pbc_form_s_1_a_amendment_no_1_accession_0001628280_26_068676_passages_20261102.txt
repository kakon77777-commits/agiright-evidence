As a result, we have in the past not identified, and may in the future not identify, a significant acceleration in our rate of development until after it has occurred. For example, our internal estimates and our expectations regarding future models, rather than our current models, indicate that we may imminently meet our Responsible Scaling Policy’s definition of “dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI.” We have not designated any of our current models as exceeding this threshold, and we continue to assess the underlying data. We are also evaluating the implications of meeting the AI research and development capability threshold and will release further reports in the future as we set forth in our Responsible Scaling Policy. If our capabilities were to outpace our safety, alignment, and security measures, or if we were unable to anticipate the pace of such advancement, we may be unable to adequately assess or mitigate the resulting risks, which could increase the likelihood of safety incidents, regulatory intervention, or reputational harm, and could adversely affect our business, financial condition, and results of operations. In light of these uncertainties, including limits on our ability to observe the pace at which our capabilities are advancing, we have adopted, and may continue to adopt, precautionary safety, alignment, and security measures to allow our safety efforts to advance together with model capabilities.
