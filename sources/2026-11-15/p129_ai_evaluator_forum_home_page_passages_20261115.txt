Learn About Our Mission Meet Our Members Read our Path Ahead Who we are The AI Evaluator Forum is a network of organizations conducting independent, third-party evaluations of AI systems. The Forum brings together practitioners to share methods, align on norms of professional practice, and inform the broader standards and policy ecosystem. The Forum is not a legal entity in its own right and relies on voluntary participation from its members. Why we exist As AI systems grow more powerful and widely deployed, society faces critical questions about their capabilities, limitations, and risks. Answering those questions credibly requires rigorous assessment by organizations independent of the systems they evaluate.
