Mr. Huang,

I am writing to request that you voluntarily appear before the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs on December 3, 2026 for a public hearing regarding recent public reports that raise significant national security concerns.

The Committee’s authority to conduct this inquiry is well-established. Pursuant to Rule XXV, paragraph 1(d)(1), of the Standing Rules of the Senate, the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs has jurisdiction over matters relating to “export controls,” including their application to NVIDIA’s exports of advanced semiconductors. The Committee’s oversight role is further recognized in the Export Control Reform Act of 2018, which expressly requires the President to notify the Committee in advance of any proposed amendments to the Export Administration Regulations and provide an explanation of their intent and rationale.

Rule XXVI, paragraph 8(a), directs the Committee to conduct continuing oversight of the administration and implementation of laws within its legislative jurisdiction. Rule XXVI, paragraph 1, further authorizes the Committee to investigate matters within that jurisdiction and to obtain testimony and documents.

(Footnote 1 cites two New York Times articles of October 29 and November 6, 2026 and a Wall Street Journal article of November 11, 2026. Footnotes 2 to 5: Standing Rules of the Senate, Rule XXV, para. 1(d)(1), item 6 (assigning jurisdiction over export controls); Export Control Reform Act of 2018, Pub. L. No. 115-232, § 1762(c), 132 Stat. 2208, 2231 (codified at 50 U.S.C. § 4821(c)); Rule XXVI, para. 8(a) (requiring continuing review of the application, administration, and execution of laws within a committee’s legislative jurisdiction); Rule XXVI, para. 1 (authorizing investigations into matters within a committee’s jurisdiction, hearings, and the production of testimony and documents).)
