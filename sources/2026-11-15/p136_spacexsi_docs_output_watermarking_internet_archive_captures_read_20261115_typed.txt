Watermarking embeds an invisible signal in text and audio generated by Grok models, so the output can later be identified as AI-generated. Watermarking can help enable compliance with the EU AI Act. Text watermarking is off by default. Team admins can turn it on per model in the SpaceXSI Console's team settings; see How to enable text watermarking . Audio from the voice APIs is watermarked in some regions, when your team turns it on, and when you ask for it; see Audio watermarking .

Audio section, as read:
Audio watermarking Audio from the voice APIs can also include an invisible watermark. This applies to Text to Speech and Speech to Speech , including speech in custom voices . The watermark is AudioSeal . It is added to the audio before it is encoded, so every output format includes it, and it can't be heard. Which audio is watermarked Audio is watermarked when: The end user is in the EU, the EEA, or California. The location comes from the IP address of the request. Your team turned watermarking on for the voice model in the SpaceXSI Console. See Turn on audio watermarking for your team . The request asks for a watermark. See How to enable audio watermarking . If none of these apply, the audio is not watermarked.
