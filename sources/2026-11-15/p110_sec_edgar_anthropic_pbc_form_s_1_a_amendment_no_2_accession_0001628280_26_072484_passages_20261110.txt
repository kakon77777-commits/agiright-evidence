As a result, we have in the past not identified, and may in the future not identify, a significant acceleration in our rate of development until after it has occurred. For example, our use of AI systems has already been accelerating our research and development, and our internal estimates indicate that we may experience a further increase in the rate of progress in AI capabilities. Because the AI research and development capability threshold under our Responsible Scaling Policy applies where we “observe or expect” a specified degree of accelerated progress, we made a determination that we have met this threshold based on what we expect from future models and our overall rate of progress, rather than on what we have observed from current models. We continue to assess the underlying data. We are also evaluating the implications of meeting the AI research and development capability threshold and will release further reports in the future. If our capabilities were to outpace our safety, alignment, and security measures, or if we were unable to anticipate the pace of such advancement, we may be unable to adequately assess or mitigate the resulting risks, which could increase the likelihood of safety incidents, regulatory intervention, or reputational harm, and could adversely affect our 35 Table of Contents business, financial condition, and results of operations. In light of these uncertainties, including limits on our ability to observe the pace at which our capabilities are advancing, we have adopted, and may continue to adopt, precautionary safety, alignment, and security measures to allow our safety efforts to advance together with model capabilities.
