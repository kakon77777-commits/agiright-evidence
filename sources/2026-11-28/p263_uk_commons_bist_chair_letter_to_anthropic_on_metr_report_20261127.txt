Dear Pip,

Subject: METR’s Report on Artificial Intelligence Research and Development
at Anthropic

I write to request a full and unredacted version of METR's Report on AI Research and
Development Acceleration at Anthropic in October 2026, published on 5 November
2026.

METR stated publicly that Anthropic redacted substantial portions of the report before
publication, including much of the evidence it considered “most informative”.

Given the Committee's ongoing work on AI safety, I would be grateful if you could
provide the Committee with a copy of the unredacted report or otherwise make
available the material omitted from the published version, and explain the basis on
which the redactions were made.

I would be grateful for a response by Friday 4 December.

                                                Yours sincerely,

                               Rt Hon Liam Byrne MP
