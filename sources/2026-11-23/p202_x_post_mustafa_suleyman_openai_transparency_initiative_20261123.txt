We fully support OpenAI's transparency initiative. Thanks to @merettm @markchen90 @gdb @sama for leading the way.

We all got into this field to build safe AGI that is genuinely good for humanity. One of the hardest parts of AI safety is coordination, and this is a step in the right direction. Many more steps like this will be needed to deliver safe, contained, and aligned AGI in the next few years.

We're committed to sharing our pre-training progress on a common held-out evaluation set. With OpenAI's permission, we report MAI-2 Base on their evaluation set. Its log-perplexity is 0.819, putting it in the same ballpark as the GPT-5.6 Sol base model. Our model uses the same tokenizer as OpenAI, so the numbers are directly comparable.

In the future, to be able to compare across labs we might need to report bits-per-byte (BPB) rather than perplexity since different labs use different tokenizers. We will also need different evaluation datasets - the fact we have access to OpenAI's evaluation dataset is a special case.

Safety and alignment is now the mission. It's the absolute number one most important focus for our industry. And it begins with small, practical steps like these towards better coordination on pacing.
