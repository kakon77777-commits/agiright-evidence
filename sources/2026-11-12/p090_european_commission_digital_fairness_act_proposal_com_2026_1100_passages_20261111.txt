Article 17 Addictive design 1. Traders that design, organise or operate their digital products in a manner that is intended, or can reasonably be foreseen to encourage compulsive or excessive use, shall make available to consumers, in an easily accessible manner, effective tools enabling them to disable, individually or all at once, the following features that encourage such use: (a) features enabling automatic play of content and uninterrupted content consumption without effective and regular interruption moments that enable deliberation by the consumer on their willingness to consume more content; (b) notifications not triggered by, or not directly related to, the consumer’s interaction or activity on the service; (c) features incentivising engagement at regular times or with greater frequency, including through penalties or loss of benefits for failing to engage regularly or within specified time intervals; (d) features and system behaviours that encourage continued use by simulating interpersonal relations, such as persistent prompts to continue engaging with an automated system. This Article does not apply to traders subject to Articles 9, 14 and 15 of Regulation (EU) [EU KIDS Act] for practices concerning minors, nor to traders providing services falling within the categories specified in Article 2 (4) points (c), and (e) of Regulation (EU) [EU KIDS Act]. Article 26 Automated systems for contracting on behalf of a consumer with a trader 1. Traders making available to consumers automated systems that can be used by consumers to conclude and perform contracts on the consumers’ behalf with those traders or other traders shall ensure that such automated systems contain easily accessible, understandable and effective tools, enabling the consumers to express their choices and set and adjust the parameters of such automated systems referred to in paragraph 2. Consumers shall have the possibility to modify the expressed choice and change the parameters referred to in paragraph 2 at any time through the tools provided for that purpose in accordance with this paragraph. 2. Traders making available to consumers automated systems that can be used by consumers to conclude and perform contracts on the consumers’ behalf shall ensure that on the first use of an automated system, consumers are required to expressly choose one of the following: (a) whether they wish to confirm all contracts proposed by the automated system before being bound by such contracts; (b) whether they wish to confirm only a certain type of contracts, based on specific criteria to be set by the consumer, including a maximum price and a time limit for the applicability of this choice; (c) whether they wish no confirmation of contracts before being bound by them. 3.
