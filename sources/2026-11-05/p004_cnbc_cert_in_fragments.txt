The Artificial Intelligence Security Auditing Guidelines come amid growing concerns over cybersecurity risks linked to AI systems and prescribe a risk-based framework under which AI systems would be classified as low, moderate, high or critical risk.
The guidelines state that “AI agents shall operate under least privilege”
the framework specifically calls for a “kill switch mechanism”.
The guidelines also prescribe a six-hour reporting window for AI-related security incidents to CERT-In
Facts the entry paraphrases (not quoted): AI agents should not automatically inherit permissions from users, tools or other agents; critical-risk systems would face continuous monitoring and independent audits by CERT-In-empanelled organisations; audits would also examine hallucination mitigation and harmful, biased or copyright-protected outputs; labelling or watermarking of synthetic content depending on use; the framework covers the AI lifecycle from training and development to deployment and monitoring.
