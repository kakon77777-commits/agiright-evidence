 TL;DR
 Google employees are testing a new Gemini 4 variant internally codenamed Carbon, deployed across the company's Jetski development environment in recent days.
 One Google developer told Business Insider that Carbon's coding performance 'feels comparable to Anthropic's Opus 5.5.'
 Google's internal Barium-B build was the one ultimately shipped publicly as Gemini 4 Argon; Carbon's eventual public name is unconfirmed.
 Editor's note 
 If Carbon ships close behind Argon, procurement teams should assume a shorter honeymoon on whichever Google model they just standardized on, and Anthropic has to defend the Opus comparison before it hardens into received wisdom.
 Google employees are testing a new Gemini 4 variant internally codenamed Carbon, deployed across the company's Jetski development environment in recent days, Business Insider reports .
 One Google developer told the outlet that Carbon's coding performance "feels comparable to Anthropic's Opus 5.5." That is one anonymous staffer, speaking to one reporter, about an internal build nobody outside the company has evaluated. It is the kind of quote that drives procurement conversations anyway.
 The naming matters because Google's internal codenames do not match its public ones. Business Insider reports that the Barium-B build was the one ultimately selected for public launch as Gemini 4 Argon. Carbon is the next step along that line. Its final public designation is unconfirmed, and Google declined to comment on specific internal roadmaps.
 Carbon is landing while Argon itself is still being argued over inside Google. Benchmark scores look strong; some employees say the model is less convincing when they actually use it for coding work. Our tracker has logged 204 Google AI stories over the last 90 days , and this week's run alone — a Workspace MCP v2 release and an AI Edge Foresight launch — reads like a company moving faster than its public roadmap.
 No public benchmark for Carbon exists. No release date. No second outlet has confirmed the Opus 5.5 comparison.
 Editor's take 
 Given the Barium-to-Argon precedent, the public name on Carbon is probably not 'Carbon'; watch Google's next Gemini release notes for a lineage detail that confirms whether the shipped build is this Jetski variant or something later.
 What internal eval was the Google developer actually using when they said Carbon 'feels comparable to Anthropic's Opus 5.5'?
