 Strengthening frontier safeguards before broad availability

 Defending against misuse
 Argon is designed to refuse harmful requests while preserving legitimate, dual-use scientific research, as per our Frontier Safety Framework.
 Defending against prompt injection attacks
 Argon is our most resilient model yet against indirect prompt injections.
 Monitoring for misalignment
 We are deploying misalignment mitigations that monitor Argon’s chain-of-thought and actions and stop execution when necessary.
 Hardening systems
 We are hardening our sandboxed environments by isolating and sealing them before high-risk training or evaluations begin.
