Alibaba Cloud delisted several models on Oct 10 — but not the ones the rumor says.
What actually got pulled: deepseek-v3, v3.1, v3.2, R1, R1-0528, and the R1-distilled Qwen 7B/14B/32B. Old DeepSeek builds. Not DeepSeek as a vendor.
Kimi k2.7-code, GLM-5.2, MiniMax-M2.5 are still running on the platform. The "Alibaba kicked DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM and MiniMax off the cloud" story doesn't hold.
The real signal is quieter and more boring: every retirement notice recommends a Qwen replacement. qwen3.7-plus, qwen3.7-max, qwen3.6-flash.
That's a cloud pushing its own models through the migration path. Not the same as cutting off rivals.
