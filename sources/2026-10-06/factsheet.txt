The Order directs all departments and agencies to use the terms "Super Intelligence" and "SI" in official correspondence, public communications, policy documents, and non-statutory documents within the executive branch and to no longer acknowledge the terms "Artificial Intelligence" or "AI".
The Order directs the Assistant to the President for Science and Technology (APST) to propose a Federal definition of "Super Intelligence" and "SI" that reflects the state of this extraordinary frontier technology and identify any additional executive action necessary to implement the proposed definition.
