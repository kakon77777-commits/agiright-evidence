Chairmen Mast, Moolenaar, and Crawford Release Statement on Jensen Huang's Reinstatement
November 23, 2026
Press Release
WASHINGTON, D.C. – Chairmen Mast, Moolenaar, and Crawford released the following statement:
“The NVIDIA board’s brief internal investigation and reinstatement of Jensen Huang do not erase the national security threats and questions that must be addressed. Jensen Huang and Tim Teter have confirmed they will appear for their transcribed interviews on December 1st and December 2nd as previously planned,” said Chairmen Mast, Moolenaar, and Crawford. “We remain committed to getting answers on the mass smuggling of NVIDIA products, their diversion for Chinese military purposes, and their key role in advancing our foremost adversary’s AI capabilities.”
