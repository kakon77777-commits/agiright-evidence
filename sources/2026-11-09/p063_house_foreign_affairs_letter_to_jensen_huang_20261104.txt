           Dear Mr. Huang:

           We are writing to request that you appear before our committees for a joint, closed-door briefing
           on NVIDIA’s security protocols against foreign adversary espionage and your interactions with a
           publicly reported People’s Republic of China (PRC) intelligence asset: Megaspeed executive Alice
           Huang.

           U.S. technology and information. The issue of PRC infiltration and access to cutting-edge U.S.

           Congress determines the extent to which U.S. national security has been placed at risk.

           We formally request that you appear in room 2172 of the Rayburn House Office Building on
           November 18, 2026, to brief our committees.
