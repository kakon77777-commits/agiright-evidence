Agentic AI call for evidence










Start date 
8 October 2026 


Closing date 
20 November 2026 


Type 
ICO consultation 


Status 
Open 






It is open














The Information Commissioner’s Office is calling for evidence to inform our thinking on how to apply data protection law to the distinctive capabilities and risks of agentic AI .
Your responses will help us identify where we can provide greater clarity or practical support before we publish the final version of the guidance.
We welcome your feedback on the questions in this survey. They are split into the following sections:
Section 1: About you and your organisation
Section 2: Data Security
Section 3: Transparency
Section 4: Accountability
Section 5: Automated Decision Making (ADM)
Section 6: Fairness and purpose limitations
Section 7: Lawfulness of Processing
Section 8: Additional questions








How to respond
You can respond to the call for evidence through Citizen Space . 
The consultation will remain open until the end of 20 November 2026 . We may not consider responses received after this deadline.








Why your views matter
Privacy statement
For this consultation, depending on your preference, we may publish responses received from organisations in full or publish a summary of responses. If we publish consultation responses, we will remove email addresses, telephone numbers and other personal information where appropriate. Please do not include information in your response that you would not be content for us to make publicly available.
If we receive a request under freedom of information legislation for your response, we will consider the request in accordance with the relevant legislation. Where appropriate, we may contact you for your views before making a disclosure decision.
For more information about how we use personal information, please read the ICO privacy notice section on responding to our consultations and surveys .
We use Citizen Space to collect consultation responses on our behalf. Citizen Space is supplied by Delib. Please see the D elib privacy policy for further details.
