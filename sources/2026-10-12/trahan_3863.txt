Trahan, Massachusetts Delegation Statement on Mass General Brigham Nursing Contract Negotiations





Share on Facebook 


Share on X 



Print this Page 


Share by Email 





LOWELL, MA,
October 7, 2026




















Today, Congresswoman Lori Trahan (MA-03) , Senators Edward J. Markey (D-Mass.) and Elizabeth Warren (D-Mass.) and Representatives Richard E. Neal (MA-01) , James P. McGovern (MA-02) , Katherine Clark (MA-05) , Seth Moulton (MA-06), Ayanna Pressley (MA-07) , Stephen F. Lynch (MA-08) , and Bill Keating (MA-09) issued the following statement urging Mass General Brigham (MGB) and the Massachusetts Nurses Association (MNA) to continue their negotiations to avert a strike at Brigham and Women’s Hospital:

“Nurses are the backbone of our healthcare system—our loved ones and communities rely on them each and every day. Our nurses deserve fair contracts that reflect that standard of care. Their voices must be heard.”

“The nurses at Brigham and Women’s Hospital have demonstrated their willingness to continue negotiating with Mass General Brigham leadership to arrive at an agreement that works for nurses, patients, and our hospitals. A strike has the potential for serious impacts on our communities, patients, and healthcare workers, which we know no nurse or patient wishes to experience.”

“We call on Mass General Brigham to remain at the bargaining table with the Massachusetts Nurses Association and work to reach a good faith agreement that addresses the needs of nurses, patients, and our constituents. Massachusetts is renowned for its healthcare system, in which Mass General Brigham and its nurses are crucial partners, and we hope the parties can reach an agreement.”
