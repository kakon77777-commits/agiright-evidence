Frontier Safety Assessment We evaluated Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon in line with the Frontier Safety Framework (April 2026) and reported the results in the respective Safety and Security Reports ( Argon , Carbon ). Gemini 4 Hydrogen is part of the Gemini 4 series of models. We conducted a Material Capability Change (MCC) assessment to understand whether Gemini 4 Hydrogen has meaningful new capabilities or material increases in performance relative to the most recent model version subject to a critical capability assessment, which was Gemini 4 Argon. Gemini 4 Hydrogen did not show a material increase in general capability under our MCC assessment compared with Gemini 4 Argon and therefore a full suite of critical capability evaluations was not required in line with our FSF. As we could not rule out that Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon reached the CBRN CCL for biological information risk and the Cybersecurity CCL, we conducted targeted critical capability assessments of Gemini 4 Hydrogen in the CBRN and cyber domains to understand the inherent risk of the model, and found that Gemini 4 Hydrogen does not reach the CBRN or cyber CCLs, though it reaches the CCL alert thresholds for both domains.
