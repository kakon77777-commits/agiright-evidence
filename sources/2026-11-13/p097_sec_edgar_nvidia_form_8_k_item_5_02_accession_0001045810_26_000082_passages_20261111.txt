Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. On November 11, 2026, the Board of Directors, or the Board, of NVIDIA Corporation accepted the resignation of Jen-Hsun Huang, as NVIDIA’s President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board, effective immediately. Following Mr. Huang’s resignation, the Board appointed NVIDIA’s Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer Colette M. Kress, age 59, to serve as interim Chief Executive Officer, while the Board conducts a search for a new Chief Executive Officer. Ms. Kress has served as NVIDIA’s Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer since 2013 and will continue in that role while she serves as the interim Chief Executive Officer. On November 11, 2026, the Board appointed Harvey C. Jones, age 73, as Chair of the Board. Mr. Jones has served on the Board since 1993. He has been the Managing Partner of Square Wave Ventures, a private family office, since 2004 and has over 30 years of experience as an entrepreneur, high technology executive, and venture investor.
