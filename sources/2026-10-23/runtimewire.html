Aleksander Madry returns to OpenAI to work on AI safety and security

Madry says he will join Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki after concluding that increasingly capable AI demands a stronger focus on safety.

By Ryan Merket · Published Oct 19th, 2026, 1:19pm CT · Updated Oct 22nd, 2026, 6:46pm CT

Primary source: X

Why it matters

Madry returns with prior experience leading OpenAI's Preparedness team just as the company is describing safety and security risks that extend from model evaluations into training environments and tool use.

Aleksander Madry is returning to OpenAI, a few months after leaving, to work with Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki on the safety and security risks of increasingly capable AI.
