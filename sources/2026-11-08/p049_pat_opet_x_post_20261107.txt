here’s an example
[attached image, read from a zoomed screenshot, not by text extraction:]
FORTIFY THE FRONT DOOR
Sign in at the edge, before anyone reaches the system.
An Auth Filter moves sign-in to an independent security layer in front of the system, so unverified traffic never reaches it.
01 WITHOUT AN AUTH FILTER
The system itself is the front door.
Employees and customers / Attackers and AI-driven scanning (labels on the two people icons at the left of each panel)
Anyone on the internet can reach the sign-in page. One unpatched flaw there can open a path into the enterprise before anyone has signed in.
02 WITH AN AUTH FILTER
Sign-in happens at the edge, and the system is hidden.
Independent security layer at the edge. The only part reachable from the internet.
01 Screen: Block known attacks, floods and bad bots
02 Verify: Sign in with multi-factor authentication
03 Authorize: Allow only people approved for this system
Stopped at the edge: Exploit attempts, bot traffic and anyone who cannot sign in
Protected system: Hidden from the internet. Accepts connections only from the security layer.
Direct connections to the system are refused
Less exposure: The system cannot be reached directly from the internet.
An independent layer: A flaw in the system is not enough on its own to get in.
No single point of failure: Build the layer with redundancy and a tested fallback.
