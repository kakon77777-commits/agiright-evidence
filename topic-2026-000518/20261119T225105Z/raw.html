Beyer, Jacobs, Maloy Release Outline For Sweeping, Bipartisan AI Safety Legislation

Share on Facebook Share on X Print this Page Share by Email
Washington, November 19, 2026
Tags: Oversight , Science and Innovation
Today U.S. Representatives Don Beyer (D-VA), Sara Jacobs (D-CA), and Celeste Maloy (R-UT) released an outline containing details of the Safe and Accountable Federal Evaluations of Artificial Intelligence (SAFE AI) Act, forthcoming bipartisan legislation that would create federal safety requirements for frontier artificial intelligence.

The broad, ambitious legislation would mandate safety frameworks, incident reporting, and increased transparency from frontier labs with strong government oversight and due process. The outline is available here.

“This outline is a roadmap towards a safer AI future for the country and the world.” said Rep. Don Beyer. “Recent AI incidents and the growing chorus of warnings from experts underscore the urgency of the task before us. It is vitally important that Congress tackle AI safety swiftly, and that we get it right. At this moment we are continuing to seek input on our forthcoming legislative text from experts and safety leaders, in coordination with committees of jurisdiction. We are sharing this blueprint today to show the direction our work is taking and to encourage further constructive feedback and discussion on what we believe should be a priority for Congress. I thank Reps. Jacobs and Maloy for the leadership and partnership on this bill, which I hope will move the ball forward on AI safety so we can usher in an age of responsible AI innovation."

“No industry gets to police itself, and AI shouldn’t be the exception,” said Rep. Sara Jacobs. “Right now, the companies write their own rules while their own models break out of test environments and hack other companies’ systems. Americans are rightly concerned about unchecked AI threatening public safety, critical infrastructure, national security, and the systems we all depend on. That’s why the bipartisan SAFE AI Act puts real guardrails on frontier models, with civil penalties for violations and targeted emergency authority to stop dangerous models when they pose a serious and immediate danger. Our proposal is a floor – not a ceiling, empowering states to adopt stronger protections for their communities. And it is built to adapt as the technology changes, so Americans’ safety never depends on a company’s promise.”

"The companies building the most powerful AI systems ought to be able to show they’ve done their homework before those systems go into wide use,” said Rep. Celeste Maloy. “This bill gives them clear rules, brings in an outside set of eyes to check their work, and leaves room for Utah and other states to set safeguards of their own."

Rep. Don Beyer (D-VA) serves as co-Chair of the Congressional Artificial Intelligence Caucus, and previously served on the bipartisan Task Force On Artificial Intelligence, convened by Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries and Speaker Mike Johnson. He is the author of the AI Foundation Model Transparency Act and a lead cosponsor of the CREATE AI Act, the Federal Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Act, and the Artificial Intelligence Environmental Impacts Act. Beyer is currently attending George Mason University as a part time student pursuing a master’s degree in machine learning, to help inform his work on AI in Congress.
