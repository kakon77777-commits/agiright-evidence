Our Members

Credible oversight of frontier AI cannot rest on a handful of organizations. We work to broaden the field so that no single evaluator carries the weight of public trust alone.

The AI Evaluator Forum brings together independent research organizations focused on a range of issues.

Governing Members

Governing (voting) members are organizations that conduct and publish rigorous independent technical evaluations of general-purpose AI systems in the public interest.

Active Site

Providing empirical evidence on biological risks from frontier technology.

AI Verification and Evaluation Research Institute

Advancing effective third‑party auditing for frontier AI.

Apollo Research

Evaluating frontier models to reduce loss-of-control risks from AI scheming.

Collective Intelligence Project

Embedding democratic global guidance into the development and evaluation of frontier AI.

Common Sense Media’s Youth AI Safety Institute

Setting standards, conducting research, and independently testing the AI products children use most.

Meridian Labs

Building open source tools and technology for evaluating and understanding frontier AI.

METR

Evaluating frontier AI systems' ability to complete complex tasks without human input.

RAND

Developing advanced AI evaluation methods and testing systems for dangerous capabilities.

SaferAI

Developing quantitative risk models and contributing to international AI standards.

SecureBio

Securing the future against catastrophic pandemics, including by evaluating biosecurity-relevant AI capabilities.

STABL

Maintaining the standardized, cost-aware, and third-party leaderboard for evaluating agents.

Transluce

Building open, scalable technology for understanding AI behaviors and their effects on society.

Associate Members

Associate (non-voting) members support the Forum's work on shared best practices through their expertise in AI standards and governance.

Center for Democracy & Technology

Advancing AI auditing, accountability, and safety through its AI Governance Lab.

Guidelight AI Standards

Defining AI safety practices and assessing companies' implementation of them.
